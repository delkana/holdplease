# Hold, Please

A manual telephone switchboard. Verity Exchange, position 4, four in the afternoon.

Eight cord pairs, twenty-four lines, three trunks out of town. One of you.

## The job

A lamp lights when a subscriber lifts their receiver. Drag the **front plug** of a free
pair into that jack, throw the pair's **listening key** — you cannot hear a word without
it — and take the number. Then take the **back plug**, hold it over the wanted jack for a
moment to **flick-test** it (a double click means that line is busy elsewhere on the
board; there is no other way to know), plug in, and **hold the ringing key** until they
answer. Then release the listening key. The call is no longer yours.

The two small lamps beneath each pair are supervisory. Lit means that end is on the hook.
Both lit means the call is over — pull the cords and get your pair back.

Eight pairs is the whole of your capacity. Cords that cross will snag when you reach for
them, and nobody will tell you that you have snarled the shelf.

## What the board asks of you

- **Party lines.** Some wires carry two or three households. A caller asks for a *person*,
  and you tap that household's ring code out on the ringing key — a long hold, a quick tap.
  Fetch the wrong neighbour to the telephone and it goes on your sheet.
- **Toll trunks.** Out-of-town calls go over one of three trunk circuits, and other
  positions are competing for them. When all three are taken, take your cords down: that
  files a slip on the **call-back rail**, and when a trunk clears you ring that subscriber
  *back* — the only time the board works in reverse.
- **The listening key.** It is against the rules to leave it thrown once a call is
  connected. It is also the only way to hear anything. Miss Pryor walks the aisle.

## A career

Each shift is five real minutes and five hours on the clock, four in the afternoon through
to nine. Every night runs **5% thicker than the one before**, and the exchange has a bad
week on a rota — a pair of cords away for repair, a trunk tied up all evening, somebody who
settles in for two hours — alongside whoever happens to telephone that night.

Lose five calls and the chief operator takes you off the position. The run ends there, and
the sheet remembers how far you got.

## Signing on

- **Learner** — somebody at your shoulder. The board marks the busy lines and rings the one
  they asked for, and nothing goes on your sheet.
- **Operator** — you work the position yourself.
- **No allowances** — nothing is shown and nothing is forgiven.

The pace is identical in all three. Only the help changes.

## Running it

The game is a single self-contained HTML file with no build step and no dependencies.
Open `index.html` in a browser, or:

```
npm start
```

Plays with a mouse or on a touch screen (hold it sideways). `H` for the operating
instructions, `Escape` for the menu.
